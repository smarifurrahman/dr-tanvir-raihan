Dr. Tanvir Raihan

MBBS – Sir Salimullah Medical College, Dhaka
BCS Health Cadre Officer

Dr. Tanvir Raihan is a dedicated medical professional and a BCS Health Cadre Officer of Bangladesh. He completed his MBBS from Sir Salimullah Medical College, Dhaka.

He has completed 2 years of FCPS training in Internal Medicine under the Bangladesh College of Physicians and Surgeons (BCPS). During this period, he also worked as a trainee in Gastroenterology, gaining clinical experience in the diagnosis and management of gastrointestinal and liver diseases.

In addition, he has completed a Certificate Course in Diabetology (CCD), strengthening his expertise in the prevention, diagnosis, and management of diabetes.

Special Training & Expertise

  • Medicine (General Medicine)
  • Diabetes
  • Hormonal Disorders
  • Rheumatologic Diseases (Joint Pain / Arthritis)
  • Skin & Sexually Transmitted Diseases
  • Chest Diseases (Respiratory Problems)
  • Mother & Child Health
Dr. Raihan is committed to providing quality healthcare with professionalism, compassion, and evidence-based medical practice.
